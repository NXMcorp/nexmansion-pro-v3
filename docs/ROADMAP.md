# NexMansion Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Bali MVP (current)
- 10 curated Bali villas
- Luxury editorial website
- Search & filtering (dates, guests, bedrooms, amenities, price, region, features)
- Server-authoritative pricing
- Availability & double-booking prevention
- Instant-book & request-to-book flows
- Stripe integration structure + demo mode
- Traveller dashboard (trips, wishlist, messages, payments, profile)
- Host dashboard (properties, bookings, calendar, revenue, verification)
- Admin operations (properties, hosts, users, bookings, payments, concierge, support, disputes, settings, audit)
- Reviews (only after completed stays)
- Concierge form & queue
- Verification workflow and Nex Verified badge
- Trust & safety panels
- Legal pages (privacy, terms, cookies)
- SEO, sitemap, robots metadata
- Unit tests for pricing domain

## Phase 2 — Bali growth
- 25–50 properties
- Stronger host analytics
- iCal import/export for hosts
- Automated property onboarding wizard
- Rich concierge provider network
- Referral codes
- NexCoin loyalty framework (feature-flagged)
- Email notification templates
- Real Stripe webhook handling
- Host mobile-friendly calendar
- Payout tooling

## Phase 3 — International collections
- Dubai, French Riviera, Mykonos, Maldives, Saint-Barth
- Multi-currency display and settlement
- Localised content in EN/FR/ES
- Region-specific legal & registration fields
- Local concierge partner networks

## Phase 4 — Advanced
- Crypto payments (flagged provider)
- NexCoin loyalty (rewards only, never speculative)
- AR/VR tour embed
- AI concierge assistant (augments, never replaces, human concierge)
- Dynamic pricing suggestions (host tool, not automatic)
- Native mobile apps
- Advanced fraud detection
